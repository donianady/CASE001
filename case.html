CASE001-THE STOLEN SCARAB

At 11:47pm, during Nile Heritage Museum in Cairo, a priceless ancient Egyption scarab mysteriously disappeared from a locked display case
There were no signs of forced entry, the alarm remained silent, and none of the witnesses saw anyone take the aircraft

The Missing Artifact

  • Object: The Golden Scarab
  • Estimated Age: Over 3000 years
  • Material: Gold and lapis lazuli
  • Last Seen: 11:46pm
  • Discovery of Theft: 11:52pm

The Crime Scene

The scarab was displayed inside a locked glass case in the museum's perivate exhibition room. The case showed no visible damage, and the security system recorded no authorized opening during the suspected period. Six people had access to the exhibition wing that night.

Your Mission

Your task is to examine the evidence, investigate the suspects, reconstruct the timeline, and discover how the scarab disappeared. Every suspect has something to hide, but not every secret is connected to the crime.
