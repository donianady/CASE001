CONFIDENTIAL RECORDS

THE SUSPECTS

Six people has access to the exhibition wing on the night of the theft. Exammine their profiles carefully.

1.Omar Mahmoud

Role:The Museum Curator|Age 48

Background:Omar has managed the museum's private collection for twelve years. He knows every artifact and every security procedure.

Possible Motivate:The museum management discovered irregularities in its collection records.

Alibi:He claims he was in his office reviewing inventory records.

Suspicious Detail:His office keycard was used near the exhibition wing.

2.Dr.Layla Elmasry

Role:The Archaeologist|Age34

Background:Layla is an archaeologist who has studied the Gpolden Scarab for years, and maybe she has beed obsessed with it.

Possible Motivate:She belives the scarab belongs in an archaeological research institute, not a private museum.

Alibi:She says she was giving an interview in the museum's main hall

Suspicious Detail:A witness heard her arguing with Omar earlier that night.

3.Karim El-Morsy

Role:The Private Collector|Age52

Background:Karim owns a large collection of ancient aircrafts and attended the exhibition as an invited guest.

Possible Motivate:He previously offered to buy the scarab, but the museum rejected his offer.

Alibi:He claims he was speaking to guests near the entrance

Suspicious Detail:He asked a security guard when the exhibition room would be empty.

4.Youssef Ayman

Role:The Museumm Security Officer|Age29

Background:Yousef has worked at the museum for 4 years and is responsible for monitoring the exhibition wing.

Possible Motivate:He recently received a warning for a serious security mistake and fears losing his job.

Alibi:He claims he was checking the east corridor cameras

Suspicious Detail:The camera footage from part of his shift is missing

5.Mariam Othman

Role:The Artifact Conservator|Age31

Background:Mariam specializes in preserving delicate ancient objects and helped prepare the scarab for exhibition.

Possible MotivateShe disagreed with the decision to display the scarab under bright exhibition lights.

Alibi:She says she was working in the conservation labratory.

Suspicious Detail:She had handled the scarab shortly before the exhibition opened.

6.Adam Nasr

Role:The Journalist|Age27

Background:Adam was invited to cover the exhibition for a cultural news magazine.

Possible Motivate:He was investigating rumors of illegal artifact trading connected to wealthy collectors.

AlibiHe claims he was interviewing a guest near the museum entrance.

Suspicious Detail:His notebook contains a sketch of the exhibition room's layout
